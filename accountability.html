Accountability & Redress

Was an AI-made decision wrong? You can contest it. Every request is reviewed by a human — never an AI — within a 5-business-day SLA. Required for high-stakes AI under most frameworks; we offer it for all of them.

Contest an AI decision

We log each request, route it to a human reviewer, and respond within 5 business days. This is for contesting decisions — for data access or erasure, use our privacy contact. Aggregate outcomes are published here for transparency; we never publish the content of individual requests.
